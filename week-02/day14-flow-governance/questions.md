# Revision Questions and Answers

## 1. Why are approval workflows important?

Approval workflows ensure that important business actions are reviewed and authorized before execution.

They help organizations:

- Reduce errors
- Improve accountability
- Prevent unauthorized actions
- Maintain compliance
- Create audit trails

Example:

A scholarship request should be approved by the Scholarship Committee, Finance Department, and Principal before funds are allocated.

Without approvals, organizations may face financial and operational risks.

---

## 2. Why do businesses require governance?

Governance ensures that business processes follow organizational policies, security standards, and legal requirements.

Benefits:

- Protects sensitive data
- Reduces misuse
- Improves accountability
- Supports compliance
- Minimizes business risks

Governance helps organizations maintain control over critical operations.

---

## 3. What are branching workflows?

Branching workflows are automated processes that follow different paths based on specific conditions.

In Salesforce, branching is implemented using Decision Elements in Flow Builder.

Example:

If Attendance < 50%
→ Admin Escalation

If Attendance < 60%
→ Parent Notification

If Attendance < 75%
→ Warning Email

Else
→ No Action

Branching allows intelligent automation based on business rules.

---

## 4. Why should automation follow business rules?

Automation should follow business rules because organizations require consistency and control.

Benefits:

- Accurate decision-making
- Standardized processes
- Reduced manual errors
- Improved compliance
- Better customer and employee experience

Automation without business rules may produce incorrect outcomes and increase risk.

---

## 5. Why are decision nodes important in flows?

Decision Nodes determine which path a flow should follow based on specific conditions.

Functions:

- Evaluate data
- Apply business logic
- Direct process flow
- Trigger appropriate actions

Without Decision Elements, flows would not be able to make intelligent choices.

They are the core of branching automation.

---

## 6. Why should enterprises restrict sensitive operations?

Sensitive operations should be restricted to authorized users to protect organizational resources and data.

Examples:

- Budget approvals
- Salary updates
- Scholarship approvals
- Financial transactions

Restrictions help prevent:

- Fraud
- Data manipulation
- Unauthorized access
- Business losses

Role-based access control is commonly used to enforce restrictions.

---

## 7. Why are approvals important in large organizations?

Large organizations involve many departments and stakeholders.

Approvals ensure:

- Proper review
- Accountability
- Compliance
- Transparency

Approvals also prevent unilateral decision-making and reduce organizational risk.

Example:

A large budget request may require approval from Finance, Management, and Executive Leadership.

---

## 8. Why should workflows be auditable?

Auditable workflows provide a record of all actions performed within a process.

Auditability helps organizations:

- Track changes
- Investigate issues
- Meet compliance requirements
- Improve accountability

Audit records typically include:

- User information
- Date and time
- Action performed
- Approval history

Auditable workflows are essential for enterprise governance.

# Interview Tips

### What is Salesforce Flow Builder?

Salesforce Flow Builder is a declarative automation tool used to automate business processes without writing code.

---

### What is a Decision Element?

A Decision Element evaluates conditions and determines which path a flow should follow.

---

### Why are Approval Processes used?

Approval Processes ensure that important records are reviewed and approved before implementation.

---

### Why is Governance important?

Governance protects business data, reduces risks, and ensures compliance with organizational policies.

---

### What is the difference between Automation and Governance?

Automation focuses on efficiency.

Governance focuses on control, security, accountability, and compliance.

A successful enterprise system requires both.
