# AI Enhancement 1

# AI Attendance Assistant

## Problem

Faculty members manually monitor student attendance.

With thousands of students, identifying attendance risks becomes difficult.

---

## Proposed AI Solution

Create an AI Attendance Assistant.

The assistant continuously analyzes:

* Attendance Percentage
* Historical Attendance Trends
* Course Participation
* Registration Activity

---

## AI Workflow

```text
Student Attendance Data
          ↓
AI Analysis
          ↓
Risk Prediction
          ↓
Attendance Alert
          ↓
Faculty Notification
```

---

## Example

### Student A

Attendance:

```text
92%
```

AI Result:

```text
Good Standing
```

---

### Student B

Attendance:

```text
61%
```

AI Result:

```text
Attendance Risk Detected
```

Recommendation:

```text
Schedule Faculty Follow-up
```

---

## Benefits

* Early intervention
* Improved student success
* Reduced dropout risk
* Automated monitoring

# AI Enhancement 2

# AI Student Support Assistant

## Problem

Students repeatedly ask common questions such as:

* What is my attendance?
* Which courses am I enrolled in?
* What is my registration status?
* Who is my faculty advisor?

Faculty and administrators spend significant time answering repetitive questions.

---

## Proposed AI Solution

Implement an AI Student Support Assistant.

The assistant would function as a chatbot integrated into Salesforce.

---

## Example Questions

### Question

```text
What is my attendance percentage?
```

AI Response:

```text
Your attendance is currently 90%.
```

---

### Question

```text
What courses am I registered for?
```

AI Response:

```text
You are registered for:

• Salesforce Development
• Database Systems
```

---

### Question

```text
Who is teaching Salesforce Development?
```

AI Response:

```text
Faculty: Ramesh Kumar
```

---

## Benefits

* Faster support
* Reduced administrative workload
* Improved student experience
* 24/7 availability
