# Revision Questions and Answers

## 1. What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a software system that can understand requests, analyze information, make decisions, interact with tools and data sources, perform actions, and generate responses. Unlike traditional software, AI agents can reason and adapt based on user input and business context.

---

## 2. How is Agentforce different from a chatbot?

Traditional chatbots mainly provide predefined answers to user questions.

Agentforce goes beyond conversation by allowing AI agents to:

- Access enterprise data
- Trigger Flows
- Execute Apex actions
- Perform business operations
- Automate workflows

Agentforce agents can both answer questions and take actions.

---

## 3. Why do AI agents need enterprise data?

Enterprise data provides the information required for AI agents to generate accurate and useful responses.

Examples include:

- Student records
- Attendance data
- Placement information
- Employee records
- Customer information

Without access to reliable enterprise data, AI agents cannot provide meaningful business value.

---

## 4. Why should AI systems have guardrails?

Guardrails help ensure that AI systems operate safely and responsibly.

They help prevent:

- Unauthorized actions
- Incorrect decisions
- Data leaks
- Compliance violations
- Security risks

Guardrails ensure AI operates within approved business rules and policies.

---

## 5. What risks exist in autonomous systems?

Autonomous systems can introduce several risks, including:

- Hallucinations
- Incorrect automation
- Privacy breaches
- Security vulnerabilities
- Bias
- Incorrect approvals
- Over-automation

These risks require governance and monitoring to manage effectively.

---

## 6. Why should enterprises carefully validate AI actions?

AI systems can make mistakes or misinterpret requests.

Validation ensures that:

- Actions are accurate
- Business rules are followed
- Compliance requirements are met
- Risks are minimized

Critical actions should often include human review and approval.

---

## 7. How can AI integrate with Flows and Apex?

AI agents can invoke Salesforce automation tools.

### Flow Integration

Used for:

- Notifications
- Approvals
- Record updates
- Business process automation

### Apex Integration

Used for:

- Custom business logic
- Complex calculations
- External integrations
- Advanced processing

This allows AI agents to participate directly in enterprise workflows.

---

## 8. Why is AI becoming important in enterprise software?

AI helps organizations:

- Improve productivity
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Enhance decision-making
- Provide better customer experiences
- Increase operational efficiency
- Scale business processes

As enterprise systems become more complex, AI helps organizations manage information and workflows more effectively.

---

# Key Takeaways

- AI agents are more capable than traditional chatbots.
- Enterprise AI requires reliable data and governance.
- AI can integrate with Salesforce Flows and Apex.
- Human oversight remains essential.
- Responsible AI adoption balances automation with control.
- Agentforce represents the future direction of enterprise software systems.
