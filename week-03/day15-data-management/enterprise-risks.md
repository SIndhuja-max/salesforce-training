# Enterprise Risks of Incorrect Data Imports

## Scenario

Suppose 50,000 student records are imported incorrectly into Salesforce.

Even a small mistake at this scale can create major business problems across the organization.

---

# 1. Wrong Notifications

## Problem

Students receive incorrect emails or SMS messages.

Examples:

- Wrong exam schedules
- Incorrect fee reminders
- Wrong event announcements

## Impact

- Student confusion
- Increased support requests
- Loss of trust

---

# 2. Attendance Errors

## Problem

Attendance records are imported incorrectly.

## Impact

- Incorrect eligibility calculations
- Unfair academic decisions
- Student complaints

---

# 3. Fee Management Issues

## Problem

Fee records are linked incorrectly.

## Impact

- Wrong payment status
- Financial disputes
- Revenue tracking errors

---

# 4. Reporting Errors

## Problem

Management reports use inaccurate data.

## Impact

- Poor decision-making
- Incorrect planning
- Misallocation of resources

---

# 5. Duplicate Student Records

## Problem

Students appear multiple times.

## Impact

- Inflated student counts
- Duplicate notifications
- Reporting inaccuracies

---

# 6. Compliance Risks

## Problem

Incorrect or incomplete records violate institutional policies.

## Impact

- Audit failures
- Compliance issues
- Reputational damage

---

# 7. Productivity Loss

## Problem

Employees spend time correcting errors.

## Impact

- Reduced efficiency
- Increased operational costs

---

# 8. Data Integrity Problems

## Problem

Users stop trusting the system.

## Impact

- Reduced Salesforce adoption
- Manual workarounds
- Lower productivity

---

# Prevention Strategies

- Validate data before import
- Perform testing on sample records
- Use duplicate detection
- Maintain backups
- Conduct post-import verification
- Follow governance policies

---

# Conclusion

Large-scale data import errors can affect every department in an organization. Proper validation, testing, and governance are essential to protect data quality and maintain trust in enterprise systems.
