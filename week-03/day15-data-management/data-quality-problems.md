# Data Quality Problems

## 1. Duplicate Student Records

Problem:
The same student appears multiple times.

Business Impact:
- Reporting inaccuracies
- Duplicate notifications
- Confusion during record management

---

## 2. Missing Email Address

Problem:
Student email field is blank.

Business Impact:
- Important notifications cannot be delivered
- Communication breakdown

---

## 3. Incorrect Department Assignment

Problem:
Student assigned to the wrong department.

Business Impact:
- Wrong course allocation
- Incorrect reporting

---

## 4. Invalid Attendance Percentage

Problem:
Attendance exceeds 100%.

Business Impact:
- Inaccurate academic records
- Incorrect performance evaluation

---

## 5. Duplicate Course Allocation

Problem:
Student enrolled multiple times in the same course.

Business Impact:
- Reporting issues
- Resource allocation problems

---

## 6. Invalid Phone Number

Problem:
Incorrect phone number format.

Business Impact:
- Failed communication attempts

---

## 7. Missing Student ID

Problem:
Unique identifier is unavailable.

Business Impact:
- Difficulty identifying records
- Duplicate creation risk

---

## 8. Incorrect Fee Status

Problem:
Paid students marked as unpaid.

Business Impact:
- Financial disputes
- Student dissatisfaction

---

## 9. Inconsistent Date Formats

Problem:
Different date formats across records.

Business Impact:
- Data import failures
- Reporting errors

---

## 10. Outdated Student Information

Problem:
Old contact information remains in the system.

Business Impact:
- Communication failures
- Reduced data reliability
