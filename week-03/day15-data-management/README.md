# Data Management & Data Quality

## Overview

Enterprise systems depend heavily on the quality and reliability of data. Poor-quality data can lead to incorrect reports, failed communications, duplicate records, and poor business decisions.

This project focuses on understanding Salesforce Data Management concepts, Data Loader operations, Data Migration challenges, Duplicate Prevention strategies, and Enterprise Governance practices.

## Data Management in Salesforce

Data Management refers to the process of collecting, storing, maintaining, updating, and securing organizational data.

Effective data management helps organizations:

- Improve decision making
- Increase operational efficiency
- Reduce errors
- Maintain data consistency
- Ensure regulatory compliance

---

## Salesforce Data Loader

Data Loader is a Salesforce tool used for bulk data operations.

### Features

- Insert records
- Update records
- Upsert records
- Delete records
- Export records
- Bulk data processing

### Example Use Cases

- Importing thousands of student records
- Updating contact information
- Exporting reports
- Cleaning outdated records

---

## Importance of Data Quality

Data quality ensures information is:

### Accurate
Information reflects real-world values.

### Complete
Required fields are filled.

### Consistent
Data follows standardized formats.

### Reliable
Users can trust the information.

### Valid
Data follows business rules and validation requirements.
