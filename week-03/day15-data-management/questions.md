# Revision Questions and Answers

## 1. Why is clean data important?

Clean data improves decision-making, reporting accuracy, operational efficiency, and customer communication.

---

## 2. What problems happen because of duplicate records?

Duplicate records can cause reporting errors, duplicate notifications, increased storage usage, and inaccurate business decisions.

---

## 3. Why is data migration difficult?

Data migration involves handling duplicates, missing data, inconsistent formats, invalid records, and mapping challenges.

---

## 4. What is Data Loader used for?

Data Loader is used to insert, update, upsert, delete, and export large volumes of Salesforce data.

---

## 5. Why should enterprises validate imported data?

Validation ensures that inaccurate, incomplete, or invalid records do not enter the system.

---

## 6. Why are CSV formats important?

CSV files provide a standardized format for importing and exporting large datasets efficiently.

---

## 7. What risks happen during bulk import?

Risks include duplicate records, incorrect field mapping, data loss, reporting errors, and system-wide data quality issues.

---

## 8. Why is governance important in data management?

Governance establishes standards and controls that ensure data remains accurate, consistent, secure, and reliable.
