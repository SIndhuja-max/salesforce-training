# Revision Questions and Answers

## 1. Why are debug logs important?

Debug logs provide detailed information about system execution and help developers identify, analyze, and troubleshoot issues efficiently.

---

## 2. Why is debugging difficult in enterprise systems?

Enterprise systems contain multiple interconnected components such as databases, automation, APIs, integrations, and user interfaces, making root cause identification more complex.

---

## 3. What problems happen when systems scale?

Systems may experience slow performance, database bottlenecks, increased response times, automation delays, notification failures, and resource limitations.

---

## 4. Why should components be reusable?

Reusable components reduce code duplication, improve consistency, simplify maintenance, and accelerate development.

---

## 5. Why is maintainability important?

Maintainable systems are easier to understand, debug, enhance, test, and support over time.

---

## 6. Why should developers avoid tightly coupled code?

Tightly coupled code reduces flexibility, increases maintenance effort, complicates testing, and makes future changes more difficult.

---

## 7. Why do enterprise systems require monitoring?

Monitoring helps detect issues early, track system health, analyze performance, and ensure reliable operation.

---

## 8. Why is troubleshooting an important engineering skill?

Troubleshooting enables developers to identify root causes, resolve issues efficiently, improve reliability, and maintain business continuity.
