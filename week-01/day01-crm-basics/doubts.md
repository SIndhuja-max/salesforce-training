# Day 1 Doubts and Questions with Answers

##  Salesforce and CRM

### 1. How does Salesforce handle customer data for very large companies with millions of users?

Salesforce uses cloud computing and scalable servers to store and manage large amounts of customer data efficiently.

---

### 2. How does Salesforce maintain data security and privacy?

Salesforce provides:
- User authentication
- Role-based access
- Data encryption
- Security settings and permissions

to protect customer information.

---

### 3. What are the major differences between Salesforce CRM and other CRM platforms?

Salesforce is:
- Cloud-based
- Highly customizable
- Easy to integrate
- Rich in automation features
- Widely used globally

---

##  Business Workflow Questions

### 4. What happens to inactive or rejected leads in Salesforce?

Inactive leads can be:
- Archived
- Marked as closed
- Re-engaged later through marketing campaigns

---

### 5. How are Opportunities converted into actual customers?

When a deal is successfully completed or purchased, the Opportunity is marked as closed-won and the person becomes a customer.

---

### 6. Can one customer have multiple Opportunities in Salesforce?

Yes. One customer can have multiple business deals or sales opportunities.

Example:
A student applying for multiple courses.

---

### 7. How do different departments use the same customer data?

Salesforce stores centralized data, so departments like sales, support, and marketing can access the same customer information based on permissions.

---

##  Salesforce Development Questions

### 8. What programming languages are used in Salesforce development?

Main technologies include:
- Apex
- JavaScript
- SOQL
- HTML/CSS (for Lightning components)

---

### 9. What is Apex, and why is it important?

Apex is Salesforce’s programming language used for:
- Custom business logic
- Automation
- Backend development

---

### 10. What are Lightning Components?

Lightning Components are reusable UI components used to build modern Salesforce applications.

---

### 11. How are Salesforce applications deployed in companies?

Applications are tested in sandbox environments and then deployed to production using deployment tools.

---

##  Trailhead and Playground Questions

### 12. Can a Salesforce Playground expire or get deleted?

Yes. Unused playgrounds may expire after a long period of inactivity.

---

### 13. What is the difference between Playground and Production environment?

| Playground            | Production                 |
|-----------------------|----------------------------|
| Practice environment  | Real business environment  |
| Safe for learning     | Used by actual users       |
| No real customer data | Contains real company data |

---

### 14. How do Trailhead challenges verify tasks automatically?

Trailhead checks Salesforce configurations and verifies whether required tasks are completed correctly.

---
