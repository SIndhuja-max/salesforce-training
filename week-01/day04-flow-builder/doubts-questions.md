# Doubts and Questions – Salesforce Flow Builder

## 1. Why do companies automate workflows?

### Answer
Companies automate workflows to reduce repetitive manual work, improve productivity, minimize errors, and increase efficiency.

---

## 2. What problems happen with manual processes?

### Answer
Manual processes are slow, error-prone, difficult to scale, and require more human effort.

---

## 3. Difference between Screen Flow and Record-Triggered Flow?

### Answer
Screen Flows require user interaction through screens and forms, while Record-Triggered Flows run automatically in the background when records are created or updated.

---

## 4. Why is no-code automation powerful?

### Answer
No-code automation allows businesses to automate processes quickly without requiring programming knowledge.

---

## 5. When should automation be avoided?

### Answer
Automation should be avoided for processes that constantly change, require human judgment, or involve complex decision-making.

---

## 6. How does automation improve consistency and productivity?

### Answer
Automation performs tasks the same way every time, reducing errors and saving employee time.

---

## 7. What is the role of Action Elements in flows?

### Answer
Action Elements perform operations such as sending emails, updating records, and triggering notifications.

---

## 8. Why are Decision Elements important in automation?

### Answer
Decision Elements control flow logic using conditions and help automate intelligent decision-making processes.

---

## 9. What are flow variables used for?

### Answer
Flow variables store and manage data during flow execution.

---

## 10. Why are Record-Triggered Flows commonly used?

### Answer
Because they automate processes instantly whenever records are created, updated, or deleted.
