# Manual Process vs Automated Process

# Process Chosen
Fee Payment Reminder System

---

# Manual Process

In a manual system, college staff members check student fee records regularly to identify unpaid fees.

They manually:
- Verify payment status
- Track due dates
- Prepare reminder messages
- Send emails or call students individually

---

# Problems in Manual Process

## 1. Time Consuming
Staff spend a large amount of time checking records and sending reminders manually.

## 2. Human Errors
Mistakes can happen while checking records or sending reminders.

## 3. Delayed Communication
Students may receive reminders late, causing payment delays.

## 4. High Workload
Managing reminders for hundreds of students becomes difficult.

## 5. Inconsistent Process
Some students may accidentally miss notifications.

---

# Automated Process Using Salesforce Flow Builder

Using Salesforce automation, the system automatically checks fee status and due dates.

If payment is pending:
- Reminder email is automatically sent
- Notification is sent to administration
- Student records are updated automatically

---

# How Automation Improves the Process

## Faster Processing
Notifications are sent instantly without manual effort.

## Improved Accuracy
Automation reduces chances of human mistakes.

## Better Productivity
Staff can focus on more important tasks.

## Consistent Communication
Every student receives reminders on time.

## Scalability
Automation can handle thousands of student records efficiently.

---

# Conclusion

Salesforce automation improves efficiency, accuracy, and productivity by reducing repetitive manual work. Automated systems are faster, more reliable, and easier to manage in large organizations.
