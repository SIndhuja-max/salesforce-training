# College Management System – Automation Ideas

## 1. Automatic Welcome Email After Student Registration

### Process
When a student successfully registers, Salesforce automatically sends a welcome email with student details, course information, and next steps.

### Why Automation Helps
- Saves staff time
- Instant communication
- Reduces manual errors
- Improves student experience

---

## 2. Automatic Fee Payment Reminder

### Process
If the fee due date is approaching and payment is still pending, the system automatically sends reminder emails or SMS notifications.

### Why Automation Helps
- Reduces missed payments
- Improves fee collection efficiency
- Eliminates manual follow-ups

---

## 3. Auto Generate Student ID

### Process
When admission is confirmed, the system automatically generates a unique student ID for each student.

### Why Automation Helps
- Avoids duplicate IDs
- Faster admission process
- Maintains data consistency

---

## 4. Faculty Notification When Course Seats Are Full

### Process
When the maximum number of students enroll in a course, the system automatically notifies faculty and administrators.

### Why Automation Helps
- Prevents overbooking
- Improves course management
- Enables quick decision-making

---

## 5. Automatic Attendance Warning Alert

### Process
If a student’s attendance percentage falls below the required limit, Salesforce automatically sends a warning notification to the student.

### Why Automation Helps
- Early identification of attendance issues
- Reduces manual monitoring
- Encourages student accountability
