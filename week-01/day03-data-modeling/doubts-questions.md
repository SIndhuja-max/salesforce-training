# Doubts and Questions – Salesforce Day 3

## Doubts

### 1. What is the difference between Lookup Relationship and Master-Detail Relationship?

Answer:
Lookup Relationship loosely connects two objects, while Master-Detail creates a strong dependency between parent and child records.

---

### 2. Can Formula Fields use values from related objects?

Answer:
Yes, Formula Fields can reference fields from related objects using relationships.

---

### 3. What happens if multiple Validation Rules fail at the same time?

Answer:
Salesforce displays the validation error messages and prevents saving the record until all issues are fixed.

---

### 4. Why are relationships important in Salesforce?

Answer:
Relationships help connect related business data and avoid duplication.

---

### 5. Why should calculations be automated using Formula Fields?

Answer:
Automation reduces manual effort, improves accuracy, and saves time.

---

### 6. Why should invalid data be blocked early?

Answer:
Blocking invalid data maintains data quality and prevents future business problems.
